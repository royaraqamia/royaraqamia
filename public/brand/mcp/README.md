# Brand marks for the app store

These are the publishers' own marks, used to identify the app they belong to — not our
artwork and not an endorsement.

- Source: [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org), released under CC0 1.0.
- Each brand remains the trademark of its owner; the mark is used nominatively, to say
  which service a catalogue row connects to.
- Rendered white because the app is dark-only (`--background: 240 10% 8%`).
- Referenced from `BRAND_MARKS` in `backend/services/mcp/marketplace/catalog-service.ts`
  and stored in `mcp_catalog_servers.icon_override` as a same-origin path, so a member's
  browser never fetches a third party (ADR-0162).

Canva has no Simple Icons entry, so its row keeps the monogram rather than a made-up mark.
